FAQs

What is the Verve Intimate Society? +
The Verve Intimate Society is our exclusive loyalty and rewards program. As a member, you earn points every time you shop, engage, and share — then redeem those points for discounts on future purchases. The more you spend, the higher your tier and the more luxurious the rewards.
How Do I Earn Points? +
You earn credits on your investment in artwork or digital packages from your shoot ($1 per $10 spent), joining the program (free, no credit), reviewing or posting about your experience ($50 per platform — Google, Instagram, Reddit, and more each count separately, plus $50 bonus when you add photos or video such as an artwork reveal or album unboxing), following us on Instagram ($20) or TikTok ($20), referring friends ($200 each), and giving us permission to share your images online ($60).
How Do I View My Point Balance? +
Your point balance is displayed at the top of this page when logged in, and in your account dashboard. Your balance updates in real time after every qualifying action.
How Do I Redeem My Verve Intimate Society Points? +
Head to the "Redeem Your Points" section on this page, choose your reward, and click Redeem. If you select Buy Artwork, you will be sent a link to our store containing your images where you can order artwork, prints or digital files. If you are redeeming a shoot, you will receive a unique discount code to apply at checkout on your next booking.
Will My Points Expire? +
Your credits never expire. Build them up at your own pace and redeem them whenever you're ready.
Can I Redeem Multiple Rewards in One Transaction? +
Only one discount code can be applied per order. However, you can save additional codes for future purchases — they're valid for 6 months from issue date.
Is There a Limit to the Points I Can Earn? +
No limit. You can earn as many as you like. Build them up or hold them as they do not expire.
Can I Use My Credits Toward My Next Shoot? +
Absolutely — that's what they're for. Redeem your credits for a New Shoot Credit ($295) to cover your shoot fee, hair and makeup, and one fine art print, or save up for a full Mini Shoot experience ($1,495). Head to the "Use Your Credits" section to see what's available with your current balance.
How Do I Claim Credits for a Review, Referral or Film Testimonial? +
Once you've completed your action — posted a review, referred a friend, or filmed your testimonial — simply submit it through our rewards form and we'll update your credit balance within 1–2 business days. Click here to submit your reward →

For reviews and posts, paste the link to your Google review, Instagram post, Reddit thread, or TikTok, or upload a screenshot as proof. For film testimonials, you can upload your file directly through the form or share a Google Drive or Dropbox link if the file is large. For referrals, just include your friend's name and email and we'll match it to their booking.
My Question Wasn't Answered Here. Where Can I Ask? +
We'd love to help! Reach out at info@verveintimate.com.au or DM us on Instagram. Our team typically responds within 1 business day.
